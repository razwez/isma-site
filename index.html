The method

Why it works

Immersion

You learnt your first language by hearing it, not studying it. Isma gives you lots of real Arabic in clips short enough to follow, so your ear does the work.

Spaced repetition

Each word you save comes back just before you’d forget it. Every review pushes the next one further away, so a few minutes a day keeps hundreds of words.

Together

Words come back in the sentence and voice you first heard them in, so you remember how they’re really used, not just what they mean.
