Listen first. The words follow.

Isma means “listen!”. You learn Arabic the way you learnt your first language: by hearing lots of it, understanding a little more each day.

1

Watch real Arabic

A feed of sentence-sized clips from real videos, so every swipe is something new to hear.

2

Tap any word

See what it means in that sentence, its dictionary form and root, and save it with one tap.

3

Remember it

Saved words come back in the sentence and voice you first heard them in, timed so they stick.

الْحَمْدُ لِلّٰهِ بِخَيْرٍ، وَكَيْفَ حَالُكَ أَنْتَ؟
