Copyright and takedowns

Last updated 3 October 2026

Made one of the videos in Isma and want it out? Email rawaz@theisma.app with the YouTube link. No legal paperwork needed: we’ll remove it, usually within 48 hours.

How videos appear in Isma

The videos in Isma are shown through YouTube’s official embedded player. They stay hosted on YouTube and belong to the people who made them. We never download, copy, store or edit them. Every video we use is listed, with its channel and a link, on our video sources page.

Formal notices

If you’d rather send a formal copyright notice (for example under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act or the UK’s equivalent rules), email it to rawaz@theisma.app with “Copyright takedown notice” in the subject, including:

  • your name and contact details, and who you act for if it isn’t yourself;
  • the work you own, and the YouTube link of each video in Isma that uses it;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn’t authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
  • a statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for them;
  • your signature (a typed name is fine).

Our own content

If you believe something Isma made itself (a subtitle, translation or note) infringes your rights, use the same address and we’ll look into it promptly.

Removing a video from YouTube

Taking a video out of Isma doesn’t remove it from YouTube. To have it removed there, use YouTube’s own copyright process.
